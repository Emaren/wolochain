# WOLO centralized solo-owner financial governance (PROPOSED)

## Decision and disclosure

There is **one human financial authority** for WOLO's current operation. That owner may act as custody operator, reserve reviewer, and activation approver, but this does **not** constitute independent review. Two owner-controlled Macs, two cryptographic keys, separate accounts, or AI review do not create human independence. No audit, reserve assessment, or canary receipt may claim otherwise.

This is a **source proposal**, not a ratified funded-release permit. It does not silently replace the five-human release constitution. The existing solo-owner financial-intent checker proves a real signature only; the solo maintenance runtime already implements source-bound capture, offline verification and resume. Neither authorizes a funded 8094/8095 canary automatically. Legacy settlement 8092/8093 must never serve as a new Satchel return fallback.

## Financial facts never waived by governance

A signed instruction proves intent, **not** solvency or payment. Nothing in a single-owner policy can:
- waive or replay an UNKNOWN historical claim or refund without independent recipient, amount, memo, sender and transaction-finality reconciliation;
- double-count claims, original stakes, refund records and winner liabilities;
- manufacture common-height bank evidence or an independent human reserve witness;
- bypass C03 HD/DE/Traffic/cron/containers/manual/SSH/other signer writer containment;
- substitute CI, an isolated test chain, same-host RPC or the live R25 website for independent C04/C05 solvency evidence;
- substitute a staging directory or schema-only replay for source-consistent encrypted C06 backups and actual C07 paired restoration;
- spend a funded canary before separately scoped owner enrollment, fresh signed approval and an immutable one-use receipt.

UNKNOWN remains UNKNOWN. Rollback after signing or broadcast means stopping future sends and reconciling the original signed bytes, never reversing a confirmed on-chain transaction or blindly retrying.

## Separation of mechanisms, not humans

The implementation must still use distinct technical surfaces: an owner intent-signing key, a separate native Cosmos custody keyring, a separate reviewer key, distinct vault and escrow keys, private recovery identities on separate physical owner devices, and a host-pinned off-host one-use nonce witness. These keys and machines are **controlled by one owner** and are expressly not independent human reviewers. Never expose any private key, recovery phrase, enrollment passphrase, raw recipient list or protected bank record in source, GitHub, logs or chat.

1. Verify source/host/binary exact identities, authenticated restricted web-to-native 8094-only integration, 8095 reviewer contract and absence of 8092 fallback.
2. Physically prove complete C03 writer fencing across HD/DE database capabilities, Nginx/API, Traffic, timers, user/system cron, containers, jobs and manual/remote operations. Stop only exact reviewed units under an approved source-bound permit.
3. Adjudicate C04 founder claim #889, refund claim #9645, market #261848 void stake difference of 5,000 WOLO and all hashless/UNKNOWN obligations. Freeze unresolved claims outside any canary; never assume missing hash means unpaid.

5. Capture coordinated HD/DE/settlement/authority C06 encrypted backups with writers fenced and retain source; verify content-addressed off-host ciphertext on the trusted Intel receiver and actually restore and compare C07 financial census on a bounded isolated PostgreSQL instance.
6. Disclose centralized owner control publicly with proposal version, exact bounds and abort period. Two owner keys are not a second human review.

Generate a **new versioned** C08 financial governance contract requiring distinct credentials, audited source, nonce identity, public notice, signed exact single-transaction claim/recipient/amount/fee/bank root, short expiry, one-use off-host witness and rollback/stop control.
